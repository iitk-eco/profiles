Who we are

About the Department

The Department of Economic Sciences at IIT Kanpur offers a rigorous and interdisciplinary curriculum that integrates economics with technology, preparing students to solve complex real-world problems through analytical and data-driven approaches.

  • The two-year M.Sc. Economics programme builds strong foundations in quantitative modelling, economic theory, econometrics, data analysis, and machine learning.
  • Hands-on training in industry-relevant tools such as Python, MATLAB, R, and STATA equips students with practical analytical and computational skills.
  • With 25 distinguished faculty members whose expertise spans both classical and emerging fields of economics, the department fosters research-driven learning and practical problem-solving.
  • Graduates are well prepared for careers in analytics, banking, data science, consulting, public policy, and research.
